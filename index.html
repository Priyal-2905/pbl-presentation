// 01 · Problem Statement

The Problem

The manual examination of EEG for epilepsy is a time-consuming and tedious process requiring highly skilled professionals, leading to diagnostic delays and observer variability. Current methods either lack sufficient accuracy, generalization, or are not real-time applicable. There is a critical need for an accurate, efficient automated system which can detect epileptic seizures in real-time, while maintaining high sensitivity and specificity to reduce false negatives and false alarms, enabling timely medical intervention.

Literature Review

Analyzed 10 SOTA models: Multiscale CNN , LSTM , EEGNet , Ensemble , Transfer Learning, Attention CNNs, Graph CNNs. Datasets: CHB-MIT (664h, 24 patients), Bonn EEG, TUH EEG.

Research Gap

Our CNN auto-extracts spatial & temporal features from raw EEG. Multi-class classification into 3 states — Inter-ictal, Ictal, Post-ictal — enables comprehensive monitoring across the full seizure cycle.
